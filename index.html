What every scan checks

The technical basics Google and AI tools rely on

We check what search engines and AI crawlers can read on your site, then tell you exactly what to change.

🔎

Search basics

HTTPS, page title and description length, main heading, mobile viewport and XML sitemap.

🤖

AI-crawler readiness

Whether your robots.txt lets GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and PerplexityBot read your site, and whether you publish an llms.txt summary.

🧩

Structured data & previews

Schema.org markup so machines understand your business, and Open Graph tags so shared links look right.

⚡

Mobile speed

Google PageSpeed's mobile score when Google answers. Shown next to your score, not counted in it.

Copy-paste fixes

Ready-to-use code for titles, descriptions, schema, llms.txt, robots.txt, Open Graph and sitemaps.

📈

Progress over time

Score history, plus weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and yearly reports showing what you fixed and what's new.

Questions, answered

Why is it only $10?

Our reports are automated, so we don't need an expensive team for every customer. We'd rather help thousands of small businesses at a fair price than a few at agency rates.

Does HeyRanks track what ChatGPT says about my business?

Not yet. Today we check the technical side: whether AI crawlers like GPTBot and ClaudeBot are allowed to read your site, and whether you give them clear, structured information. That makes it easier for AI tools to understand your business, but it doesn't guarantee they'll recommend you, and we won't pretend it does.

Will it renew automatically?

No. The 180-Day Pass is a one-time payment. When it ends, you can choose to renew or not. We'll never charge you without asking.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Every task is explained in plain language, and code fixes come ready to copy and paste. If you have a developer, just forward them the report.

No honest service can guarantee Google rankings or what an AI will say. We guarantee useful, specific weekly guidance. If the first 4 reports don't help, you get a full refund.

Can agencies use it for clients?

Yes. Each 180-Day Pass covers one website, and every report arrives by email so you can forward it to your clients. Buy one pass per site you want to track.
