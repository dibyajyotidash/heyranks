Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what HeyRanks (operated by DexoSoft Solutions, India) collects and how we use it. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, your password (stored only as a one-way secure hash, never in readable form) and, if you choose, your business name and a WhatsApp number. We don't send WhatsApp messages yet.
  • Business details: the websites, keywords, competitor websites, business name and customer questions you add; the results of our scans and audits; and the publicly available content of the pages we crawl on those websites.
  • Login sessions: when you log in, we record the IP address and browser (user agent) of that session to protect your account. They are deleted when you log out or the session expires, after at most 30 days.
  • Payment records: transaction ID, plan, amount and date. Card or UPI details are handled by our payment partner, not by us.
  • Basic usage data: pages visited, the website that linked to us, campaign tags in the link, device and browser type, screen width, language, time zone and approximate location (city and country, as reported by our hosting provider), for analytics and abuse prevention. We don't store your IP address for this or use tracking cookies: visits are counted with an anonymous ID that changes every day, and these records are deleted after about 13 months.

The free self-check on our homepage runs entirely in your browser, and its answers are not sent to us.

How we use it

  • To run scans and send your reports and account emails (login links, password resets, receipts)
  • To process payments and refunds
  • To improve the Service
  • To send product emails, which you can unsubscribe from at any time

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run HeyRanks:

  • Hosting and database (Vercel and Turso), payment processing (Cashfree Payments) and email delivery (Resend).
  • Google PageSpeed Insights, which receives the addresses of the website (and competitor websites) we're checking.
  • Cloudflare DNS over HTTPS (cloudflare-dns.com), which receives the domain names we look up (your website, its email records and competitor domains).
  • rdap.org and the domain registries it points to, which receive your domain name to read its public registration and expiry date.
  • Anthropic (Claude), when AI analysis is switched on: your website's address, our findings, your keywords and the public text of the pages we crawled, to write your report summary and content suggestions.
  • AI assistants used for AI-visibility checks (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Perplexity, as listed in your report), when switched on: the questions you set (or built from your keywords) and your business name and domain.
  • DataForSEO, when keyword and backlink data is switched on: your domain, your competitors' domains and your target country.

None of these receive your email address, password or payment details from us. Your report lists which of these sources were used.

How long we keep it

Account, website and payment records: while your account is open, plus 12 months for support and records, or longer where law requires (for example, tax records). Login sessions: up to 30 days. Crawled page content: 45 days (always for your latest audit). Audits, findings and visit records: about 13 months. You can delete your account and its websites, scans, audits and reports at any time from your account page; payment records are kept for accounting.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing hello@dexosoftsolutions.com. We respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access is limited to people who need it to run the Service.
