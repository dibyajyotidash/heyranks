Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what HeyRanks (operated by DexoSoft Solutions, India) collects and how we use it. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email and, if you choose, a WhatsApp number for report delivery.
  • Business details: websites, profiles, keywords and competitors you ask us to track.
  • Payment records: transaction ID, amount and date. Card or UPI details are handled by our payment partner, not by us.
  • Basic usage data: pages visited, the website that linked to us, campaign tags in the link, device and browser type, screen width, language, time zone and approximate location (city and country, as reported by our hosting provider), for analytics and abuse prevention. We don't store your IP address for this or use tracking cookies: visits are counted with an anonymous ID that changes every day, and these records are deleted after about 13 months.

The free self-check on our homepage runs entirely in your browser, and its answers are not sent to us.

How we use it

  • To run scans and send your reports and alerts
  • To process payments and refunds
  • To improve the Service
  • To send product emails, which you can unsubscribe from at any time

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run HeyRanks: hosting (Cloudflare), payment processing (Cashfree Payments), email and WhatsApp delivery, and AI model providers used to test AI visibility. Those AI providers receive your business name, category and city, not your personal contact details.

How long we keep it

While your plan is active, plus 12 months for support and records, or longer where law requires (for example, tax records).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing hello@dexosoftsolutions.com. We respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access is limited to people who need it to run the Service.
